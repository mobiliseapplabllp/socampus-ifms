3. Location Data – Detailed Disclosure

The App collects precise location data using the device's GPS through the ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION permissions.

Background location collection. The App also collects location data in the background — that is, even when the App is closed or not actively being used — using the ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission together with an Android foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION). A persistent notification is shown while background location tracking is active.

Why we collect background location

  • To record the on-duty location and movement of field/service staff during their working hours.
  • To verify on-site presence and attendance at assigned tasks and locations.
  • To enable accurate, reliable task and service tracking for the organisation.

When background location is collected

  • We first obtain your explicit consent inside the App before background location access is requested.
  • Background location is enabled only when you manually grant the "Allow all the time" permission from your device Settings. It is never enabled automatically — you have to turn it on yourself.
  • It is collected only during active work/duty sessions in the App.
  • You are shown an in-app prominent disclosure before this permission is requested.

We do not use location data for advertising, and we do not sell location data to any third party.

Demonstration video

A demonstration video showing how the App requests and uses location in both the foreground and background (including the consent and permission flow) is available here: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/8H4HmPCqQDo.

Concierge module – engineer live tracking

The App includes a module called Concierge, which provides live location tracking of engineers while they are on duty. This live tracking is performed only with the explicit consent of the engineer. The engineer is informed in advance and must manually grant the required location permission before tracking begins, and can stop tracking at any time by revoking the permission from device Settings.
